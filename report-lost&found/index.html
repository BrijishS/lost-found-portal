Quick Actions

Lost something on campus? Submit the details of your missing item, including its name, description, location, and the date it was lost. This helps other students and staff identify and return your belongings if they are found.

Found an item on campus? Share its details and the location where you discovered it. Your report helps the rightful owner identify and reclaim their lost property quickly and securely.

Browse or search through the list of reported lost and found items using keywords or filters. Check whether your lost item has already been reported or find the owner of an item you've found.
